ABOUT

About Me

I am a Computer Science and Technology student at Nanchang University.

My research interests lie in artificial intelligence, computer vision and generative models, with a focus on face image generation, diffusion models and controllable image synthesis.

I am particularly interested in exploring how generative models can be combined with structural and semantic conditions to achieve more precise image generation and editing.
