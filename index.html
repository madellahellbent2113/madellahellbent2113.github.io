🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

My iPhone isn't being detected

Make sure you're using a genuine Apple USB cable and that your iPhone is unlocked. On your iPhone, you might see a "Trust This Computer?" alert — make sure to tap "Trust." If issues persist, try restarting both your computer and your iPhone.

The app won't install

Ensure your Windows operating system is up to date. If you see a SmartScreen warning during installation, click "More Info" and then "Run Anyway" — this is just Windows checking the file's reputation.
