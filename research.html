RESEARCH

Research

My research interests focus on computer vision, generative artificial intelligence and controllable image generation.

01

Face Image Generation

I am interested in controllable face image generation and face pose manipulation using generative models.

My undergraduate research focuses on generating target facial poses while preserving identity information and image quality.

02

Diffusion Models

I work with diffusion-based generative models, including Stable Diffusion and related architectures.

My research explores how diffusion models can be adapted for more precise and controllable image generation.

03

ControlNet & Controllable Generation

I am interested in using structural conditions and control signals to guide image generation.

In my undergraduate research, ControlNet was used to provide structural constraints for facial pose generation.

04

Prompt Optimization

I also study how prompt design influences the generation process of diffusion models.

By simplifying and optimizing textual conditions, the generation process can be made more focused on the desired semantic and structural attributes.
